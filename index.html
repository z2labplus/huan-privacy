隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

中文

《环环相扣 Panda Rings》是一款可离线游玩的单机益智游戏。我们高度重视你的隐私：

  • 我们不收集任何数据。游戏不包含任何分析、追踪、广告或上报组件，不会收集、传输或分享你的任何个人信息。
  • 数据仅保存在你的设备上。游戏进度、得分与设置仅通过本地存储（localStorage）保存在你的设备中，不上传到任何服务器；卸载游戏或清除浏览器数据即被删除。
  • App 内购买由 Apple 处理。“解锁全部关卡”通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成，付款和账户信息只由 Apple 处理，我们收不到也不保存你的付款信息。是否已购买由 App 向 Apple 查询后保存在你的设备上；购买和恢复购买需要联网。
  • 每日提醒只在本机。如果你开启每日提醒，提醒由设备本地安排，不经过任何服务器。
  • 不需要账号。游戏没有注册、登录或第三方登录功能。
  • 无第三方服务。游戏不嵌入任何第三方 SDK，除上述与 Apple 的购买通信外不发送网络请求。

如果你对隐私有任何疑问，可以通过应用商店页面提供的联系方式与我们取得联系。

English

Panda Rings (环环相扣) is a single-player puzzle game you can play offline. We take your privacy seriously:

  • We collect no data. The game contains no analytics, tracking, ads, or reporting components. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • Data stays on your device. Game progress, scores, and settings are stored only on your device via local storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple. "Unlock All Levels" is an Apple in-app purchase. Payment and account details are processed only by Apple; we never receive or store your payment information. The app asks Apple whether you own the purchase and keeps that status on your device. Purchasing and restoring need an internet connection.
  • Daily reminders stay on your device. If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device and never goes through a server.
  • No account required. The game has no registration or sign-in.
  • No third-party services. The game embeds no third-party SDKs and sends no network requests other than the purchase communication with Apple described above.

If you have any questions about privacy, please contact us via the contact information provided on the App Store page.
